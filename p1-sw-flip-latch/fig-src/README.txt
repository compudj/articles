RCUDeletion.fig / RCUDeletion.eps
---------------------------------
xfig source (and its EPS render) of Figure 3, "RCU Linked-List Deletion",
from:

  M. Desnoyers, P. E. McKenney, A. S. Stern, M. R. Dagenais, J. Walpole,
  "User-Level Implementations of Read-Copy Update",
  IEEE TPDS, 2012  (bib key: desnoyers2012urcu).

Kept here as the layout provenance for fig-list-remove.tex: time on the
horizontal axis, viewpoints stacked vertically, each list drawn as a vertical
node stack. Our figure reuses that arrangement and adds the transaction and
its commit (proxies + one shared selector), which the original does not depict.
Not \input by the paper; reference material only.
